# Blackmask Monetization Plan

**Recorded:** November 20, 2026  
**Status:** Proposed revenue catalogue for product planning. The founder's payment rule is confirmed: one-time purchases except storage. Individual products, prices, fees, and delivery dates are not approved or implemented by this document.  
**Related:** [App plan](app-plan.md), [product strategy](product-strategy.md), and [onboarding/rewards](onboarding-habits-and-rewards.md).

**User income:** See the [user earning plan](user-earning-plan.md) for 16 ways people can earn, optional offer/product/task surfaces, private commissions, digital-product delivery, settlement requirements, and the later storage-provider candidate. This catalogue describes platform revenue; user proceeds, funded rewards, and platform income must remain distinguishable.

## 1. Payment model and free core

**Hosted storage is the only recurring Blackmask charge.** Software enhancements, bundles, licenses, services, and goods are one-time purchases. Marketplace commissions apply to individual completed transactions; they are not subscriptions. Donations, sponsorships, and grants are one-off awards or separately agreed finite purchases with no automatic renewal.

Keep these core capabilities independent of enhancement purchases, points, supporter status, and storage upgrades:

- Direct/group encrypted messaging and basic private communities/channels, subject to actual supported implementation.
- Core text/media encryption and protection improvements, contact/device verification, and session revocation.
- The vault's separate lock, secure local storage, basic supported credential/note/identity management, and essential recovery.
- Essential anti-phishing/account protections, block/report controls, and accessibility.
- Basic generators, ordinary search, data portability, and standard supported import/export.
- Basic local backups and user-managed storage/self-host paths where implemented.
- Ordinary use within a transparent included storage allowance; paid storage must not be a prerequisite to send normal text messages.

Free does not imply infinite hosted storage, unlimited attachment retention, or unsupported mobile features. Publish quotas and actual limits before launch.

Purchased tools and earned copies of the same enhancement provide equivalent capability. Never withhold a security fix, stronger encryption, account recovery, or device revocation to sell a paid tier.

## 5. Bundles, gifting, and ownership

Packaging strategies:

- Individual one-time tools/packs.
- Personalization, Personal Productivity, Community Builder, and Organization convenience bundles.
- A clearly bounded one-time launch collection; list exactly what is included.
- Gift purchases and gift codes, including purchases on behalf of a community.
- Choose-your-own bundles and credit for products already owned.
- Milestone points redeemable for eligible native products under the existing rewards plan.
- Optional future major-version upgrades when they add substantive new utility. Security fixes and essential protocol protection must not be withheld to force upgrades.

Define whether ownership is per account, Space, or deployment. Publish device support, restoration, transfer rules, and version coverage before sale. Preserve earned/purchased products during absence. Never describe a one-time tool purchase as unlimited lifetime hosted service.

A bundle is a sales package, not an extra charge on top of the same products.

## 6. Hosted storage: the recurring exception

Storage can be billed monthly or annually, with clear quota, billing period, renewal, cancellation, and restoration rules.

| Storage offering | What the recurring charge pays for |
| --- | --- |
| Personal encrypted storage | Additional hosted vault attachments/documents and eligible stored files |
| Shared Space storage | A pooled hosted file quota, paid by the Space/organization rather than every member |
| Encrypted backup/archive storage | Additional hosted backup capacity, retained versions, or longer eligible file retention |

Prefer understandable pooled quotas where feasible. Do not accidentally charge the same bytes twice across categories. Version history and retention consume storage; they are not separate recurring feature unlocks.

Before prices are set, calculate actual hosting, replication, backup, storage-related transfer, payment-platform, and support costs. Exact quotas, free allocation, retention, and prices remain open.

- Encrypt stored content through the approved architecture; storage payment does not buy stronger encryption.
- Show usage and notify before limits. Do not silently add automatic overage charges.
- Explain what counts: originals, thumbnails, versions, backups, shared files, and trash if retained.
- Define a cancellation/downgrade grace period and export path before launch; no immediate surprise deletion or destructive automatic downgrade.
- Define whether new uploads pause when over quota and how existing files can be read/exported.
- Explain that client-side encryption limits account-recovery options and does not eliminate server metadata.
- Preserve user-managed storage/self-host options where supported. Do not make basic export or local backup dependent on renewing storage.
- Do not claim native storage integration, reliable backup restore, or encrypted archive support until verified.

## 7. Add-on and digital-goods marketplace

Revenue candidates:

1. Sell Blackmask's own one-time extensions, packs, templates, and toolkits.
2. Allow approved independent developers/artists to sell eligible one-time products.
3. Take a disclosed platform share of each completed third-party product sale.
4. Offer creator collaboration bundles with an agreed split.
5. Charge a one-time, clearly described distribution/configuration service where useful.

A marketplace purchase must show total price, seller, capabilities, permissions, supported versions, and restore/refund behavior. Validate sandbox enforcement and review updates before products can access sensitive capabilities. Paid status is not a safety certification.

The Blackmask add-on commission has not been set. Do not silently copy a fee from a different product or charge it twice. Basic encrypted conversations must not require a marketplace purchase.

## 8. Ecosystem commerce and referrals

- Optional contextual links can lead to FBM purchases, digital goods, paid finite services, or relevant creator offerings that users deliberately open.
- Existing FBM commerce fees apply only where that transaction is actually governed by FBM's current rules. Do not invent an additional Blackmask message, encryption, or checkout toll.
- Paid community/event products can create a one-time commerce transaction; participation in private chats remains independent.
- Disclosed referral/affiliate revenue is a candidate for products a user intentionally explores in an optional catalogue. Do not infer needs from private conversations or vault contents.
- Optional one-time tips/support purchases for artists, plugin developers, community projects, or Blackmask can use explicit checkout. Any platform/payment deductions must be disclosed.

No recurring creator memberships, recurring paid channel access, non-storage server subscriptions, or recurring plugin charges under this payment rule. Hosted third-party costs must not be smuggled into an enhancement subscription.

External products have their own terms; do not present an independent provider's fee as a new Blackmask recurring offering.

## 9. One-time services and organizational sales

| Service | Sale unit |
| --- | --- |
| Self-host deployment/configuration | A defined installation or deployment project |
| Migration assistance | A defined supported import/migration project |
| Organization onboarding | A finite setup and training package |
| Workshops | A scheduled individual or group session |
| Custom design/branding | A defined deliverable |
| Custom integration development | A scoped integration project, with data/security boundaries reviewed |
| Workflow configuration | A finite automation or template implementation |
| Dedicated technical assistance | A clearly bounded support incident or block of work |
| Deployment review/hardening | A specified configuration review and remediation project; no universal security guarantee |
| Procurement/distribution package | A one-time organization rollout of permitted products and materials |

Do not create monthly retainers, mandatory maintenance fees, premium recurring support, or server-seat subscriptions. Ongoing hosted operation beyond storage requires a sustainable design compatible with the payment rule; do not promise unlimited operation with a one-time setup fee.

Follow upstream open-source licensing and attribution obligations. A commercial package is not permission to relicense inherited code, hide required source, or imply affiliation with upstream maintainers. White-label/distribution terms need review before sale.

## 10. Sponsorships, grants, donations, and physical goods

Additional candidates:

- One-time supporter purchases with optional cosmetic recognition.
- One-off donations and pay-what-you-want purchases.
- Finite crowdfunding/founder campaigns with explicit deliverables and realistic fulfillment terms.
- Grants for accessibility, privacy, open-source infrastructure, community communication, or research.
- Organization-funded feature development and sponsored open-source maintenance deliverables.
- Fixed-scope sponsorship of a free theme, learning pack, community toolkit, event, or release.
- Contextual sponsor placement in an optional public catalogue/learning area, purchased as a finite campaign.
- Physical merchandise: apparel, mask artwork, stickers, printed guides, or optional invitation/profile cards.

Sponsorship must be labeled and must not buy access to message contents, vault data, contacts, private membership, identity links, or security outcomes. Supporter recognition must not be confused with contact verification or trustworthiness.

Avoid paid placement inside private conversations, tracking-based ads, data resale, speculative tokens, recurring supporter memberships, and gambling-style paid rewards.

## 11. Suggested launch order

| Priority | Revenue strategy | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | Original themes/masks/stickers, community branding, and a small convenience bundle | Clear one-time value with little incremental hosting cost; confirm content rights and accessibility |
| First, when operations are ready | Additional encrypted hosted storage | Directly funds an ongoing resource cost; requires working quota, billing, restore, and cancellation behavior |
| Next | Vault organization/templates, community starter kits, assisted migration, and finite setup/training | Fits repeated user needs; sell only implemented/validated deliverables |
| Later | Third-party marketplace, organization deployments, custom integrations, and on-device advanced tools | Requires permissions, license, compatibility, support, and security work |
| Opportunistic | Grants, donations, sponsored deliverables, finite campaigns, merchandise | Diversifies funding without making communication depend on subscriptions |

Do not enable every revenue stream at once. Measure purchases, restoration, refunds, storage margin, and usefulness with minimal transaction/aggregate data. Do not measure conversion by inspecting messages, vault records, or pseudonymous relationships.

## User earning and security-tool boundaries

User income requires an actual buyer or funder, verified payment/entitlement state, explicit terms, and working delivery/settlement. Do not substitute points for promised cash, introduce a fee to read ordinary encrypted messages, or expose private conversations/vault data to establish a sale or dispute. The user earning plan defines the required connected flow.

The [privacy/security](privacy-security-oss-research.md) and [hidden-device](hidden-device-detection-oss-research.md) inventories are research, not paid products ready for sale. Keep essential anti-stalking/security and accessibility in the free core; optional convenience workflows remain one-time purchases. Do not sell guaranteed detection, anonymity, AI resistance, or passive storage income.

## 12. Decisions still needed

- Exact launch products and features that qualify for milestone rewards.
- Prices and bundle composition.
- Account/Space/deployment ownership scope and supported versions.
- Included storage, paid quotas, retention, downgrade grace period, and storage cost model.

These open decisions do not change the approved payment rule: **one-time purchases, with hosted storage as the only recurring charge.**
