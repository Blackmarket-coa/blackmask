# Blackmask User Earning Plan

**Recorded:** November 21, 2026  
**Status:** Proposed user earning opportunities and workflow requirements; payments, marketplaces, integrations, and payouts still require implementation and verification.

## 1. Purpose and payment rule

Users should be able to earn as creators, developers, service providers, teachers, testers, translators, moderators, and organizers without needing a large following.

Starting model: **private commissions + useful digital products + paid community labor**. Every earning opportunity needs a buyer, sponsor, or explicit funding source. Points, badges, participation, and attention are not automatically money.

Defined engagements and enhancements are one-time purchases. Hosted storage is the only recurring Blackmask charge. Ordinary encrypted communication, basic communities, vault locking, verification, essential recovery, accessibility, and standard portability remain independent of purchases.

## 3. Optional product surfaces

Keep the three-tab navigation. Earning actions appear in relevant contexts:

- An enabled profile: **Offer a service**, **Create a product**, and **Commission this person**.
- Optional Add-ons: **Publish your pack or tool**, with compatibility, permissions, ownership, and price.
- A funded Space project: **Offer a paid task** or **View funded tasks**, naming the funder and acceptance criteria.
- A paid-project conversation: **Review agreement**, **View order**, and **Deliver selected files**.
- Earnings settings: receipts, fees, seller net proceeds, and accurate payout states.
- A deliberate sharing action: send a chosen commercial product link with clear context.

These are proposed surfaces, not existing buttons. A private message or uploaded file is not permission to list it for sale. Never inspect vault contents or private conversations to infer products, buyers, or sales prompts. Users choose public content and presented identity.

## 4. Commission workflow

1. Buyer taps Commission this person or follows a deliberately shared offer.
2. Agree on deliverable, price, deadline, revision terms, and cancellation conditions.
3. Issue a versioned quote/order through the supported commerce system.
4. Buyer reviews total, identity, recipient, and provider-required verification before checkout.
5. Show authoritative payment/order state through a minimal authorized reference; never put bank/payment credentials into chat.
6. Collaborate privately with clear audience, room encryption, and connected-tool access.
7. Deliver only the selected work through a verified mechanism.
8. Buyer confirms completion or uses the defined issue/dispute path.
9. Settle according to explicitly selected payment/commerce rules; do not assume escrow, unconditional holds, or automatic splits.
10. Show receipt, deductions, seller net amount, settlement status, and failures accurately.

A message, screenshot, or animation is not proof of payment. Retries/callbacks must not duplicate charges, orders, entitlements, or payouts. Fees, holds, splits, refunds, and payout timing remain decisions to resolve.

## 5. Digital products and collaboration

Creators deliberately select the product/files, title, preview, rights, compatibility, permissions, price, delivery, and ownership terms before publishing through supported commerce.

Verify checkout and entitlement/delivery, purchase restoration, refunds, reporting, update support, and participant agreements for revenue-sharing bundles. A purchase does not authorize a plugin to access a vault. Do not promise extraction of inaccessible encrypted history as a migration service.

Feedback should describe completed work through an explicit process; it must not expose private relationships or convert message volume into reputation.

## 6. Ecosystem wiring and privacy

| Product | Intended responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Blackout | Discovery, demonstrations, participation, contribution visibility, reaching customers |
| FBM | Applicable listings, checkout, orders, commerce fees, payment/payout integration |
| Blackmask | Private inquiries, negotiations, identity/audience review, collaboration, controlled delivery |

Verify contracts joining listings, quotes, orders, entitlements, delivery, and payouts with minimal authorized identifiers. Do not duplicate the commerce ledger in messages.

Explain which identities each product receives and which are public. Linking must be explicit and revocable. Payment-provider identity requirements may differ from a public pseudonym; do not promise payment anonymity.

Existing FBM fees apply only where those rules actually govern the transaction. Blackmask's add-on commission is unresolved. Prevent double charges, show net proceeds, and do not add a fee to ordinary encrypted messaging.

## 7. Funding and settlement requirements

- Each paid task names a funder, amount, deliverable, acceptance rule, and payment mechanism.
- Purchases/funded awards generate earnings; native points remain separate unless an explicitly funded cash redemption exists.
- Eligibility does not depend on followers, buying enhancements, recruitment, or unnecessary public identity disclosure.
- Define seller setup, payout destination, provider verification, receipts, refunds, disputes, cancellations, and failed settlement.
- Keep fees, processing costs, refunds, collaborative splits, and seller net distinct.
- Store commerce evidence separately; a dispute does not authorize blanket access to a conversation or vault.
- Bots, bridges, and AI must not silently receive sensitive files or paid work.
- Expiration/revocation cannot retract work already downloaded or captured.

## 8. Storage-provider income: later candidate

Approved individuals or community infrastructure operators could eventually provide encrypted storage and receive a share of real storage revenue.

This is research, not current functionality or guaranteed income. It requires a separate design for keys/encryption, provider trust, metering, durability, replication, recovery, availability, abuse handling, costs, and payouts. Providers must not need plaintext vault contents. Do not place user data on an experimental network without explicit informed authorization.

## 9. Suggested launch order and acceptance

Prioritize digital packs/resources, custom commissions/community setup, workshops/consultations, and funded tasks. Expand into reusable kits, validated plugins, and collaboration bundles after the full flow works. Storage-provider income needs its separate review first.

- [ ] An ordinary user understands what they can offer and who pays.
- [ ] Offers, commissioning, product publication, and funded tasks are discoverable without another mandatory tab.
- [ ] Content, presented identity, and ecosystem links are deliberate choices.
- [ ] Quote → checkout → order → collaboration → delivery → settlement works end to end.
- [ ] Authoritative payment/entitlement states govern access and retries are idempotent.
- [ ] Receipts, fees, net proceeds, failed payouts, refunds, and disputes are accurate.
- [ ] Delivery does not expose unrelated vault data, payment credentials, or conversations.
- [ ] Core safety remains independent of purchases and rewards are not presented as guaranteed cash.
- [ ] Product rights, compatibility, accessibility, security, restoration, and support are defined before sale.

Open decisions: launch categories, seller onboarding, provider capabilities, fee/commission allocation, ownership, delivery/acceptance terms, dispute handling, payout timing, identity boundaries, and cross-repository implementation ownership.
